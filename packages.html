Care Packages

Send joy directly to a shelter dog’s kennel.

More Than Just Toys

Shelter life can be boring, same toys, same treats, same dogs they’ve been seeing for a while. Care packages break that routine with items that stimulate minds, soothe anxiety, and bring genuine joy. It’s important to note that if a dog gets toys then they might share it with their friends, bringing happiness to the whole community of pups.
