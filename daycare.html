🛁 Relaxation & Wellness

Doggy Spa Day

Give a shelter dog relaxation. Gentle grooming, soothing baths, and simply stress-relieving care.

Why Spa Days Matter

For shelter dogs, stress is frequent. Unfamiliar sounds, smells, and routines can overwhelm even the calmest pups. Our spa days aim to provide time for the dogs to feel fully relaxed and at peace.

💆

Reduces Anxiety

Calming environments and gentle touch lower cortisol levels.

✨

Boosts Adoptability

Clean, relaxed dogs make better first impressions.

❤️

Builds Trust

Positive human contact helps dogs feel safe again.

What's Included

Every spa day is tailored to each dog's needs and comfort level.

🛁

Gentle Bath

A warm, soothing wash with calming shampoo to cleanse their coat and spirit.

🐾

Paw Care

Nail trimming and pad moisturizing to keep their paws healthy and comfortable.

💆

Deep Massage

Therapeutic massage to release physical tension and promote deep relaxation.

🌸

Aroma-therapy

Dog-safe essential oils to create a serene and calming sensory experience.

🧴

Coat Care

Conditioning and gentle brushing for a silky, mat-free, and healthy coat.

🧘

Stretching

Gentle assisted stretching to improve mobility and help dogs feel limber.
